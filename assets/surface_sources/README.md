# Sources des surface tiles Potager (#64)

Les deux PNG `grass_reference.png` et `earth_reference.png` sont des matières peintes générées avec l'outil intégré `imagegen` à partir de la palette et du style des sprites Growstep existants. Aucune graine ni version de modèle n'a été exposée par cet outil. Ils servent de références de matière au script `scripts/author_surface_kit.py`, qui a amorcé les quatre ORA familiaux 320×160. Les ORA sont les sources éditables canoniques ; le script d'amorçage les **écrase** s'il est relancé. `scripts/build_surface_tiles.py` produit ensuite les PNG 80×40 à partir des couches ORA par downsampling déterministe et raccord de bord opaque.

Prompts utilisés avec le mode intégré :

- **Herbe** : “Create a broad, uniform, hand-painted grassy ground material for a cozy isometric mobile garden. Match soft painterly brushwork and muted sage/olive greens from the existing Growstep foliage. Very subtle woven leaf and blade texture with irregular light, no strong patches or repeated motifs. Looking straight down at a flat surface. The entire image is continuous grass with no islands, rocks, flowers, objects, shadows, borders, tile outlines, text or sky. Must be suitable for cropping into many small adjacent ground tiles without visible checkerboard. Color centered near #9DBF72, with low contrast and gentle warm highlights.”
- **Terre** : “Create a broad, uniform, hand-painted bare garden soil material, looking straight down at flat ground. Soft painterly brushwork, muted warm cocoa brown and earthy ochre, close to Growstep palette #6C503C, with extremely subtle fine granular mottling and a few quiet small brush flecks. Continuous material edge to edge with low contrast, suitable for cropping into many adjacent small isometric tiles. No grass, leaves, stones, cracks, roots, plants, shadows, borders, tile outlines, text or sky. The texture should look natural and calm rather than noisy.”
