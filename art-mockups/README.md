# Exemples d'écran Growstep

Trois maquettes exploratoires de l'écran principal, créées avec l'outil imagegen intégré à partir de [`ART_BIBLE.md`](../ART_BIBLE.md). Elles montrent une interface et une composition communes pour les trois zones :

- [`potager.png`](potager.png)
- [`jardin-fleuri.png`](jardin-fleuri.png)
- [`verger.png`](verger.png)

Ces images servent à discuter de la direction artistique. Elles ne sont pas des sprites modulaires ni des exports prêts à intégrer. La projection, les dimensions des objets, les couleurs et les états de jeu restent à vérifier contre la bible lors de la production des assets.

## Prompts de production

Le potager a été généré en premier avec ce prompt, puis repris pour corriger la projection :

> Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished PORTRAIT mobile app screenshot for Growstep, the POTAGER vegetable-garden screen. No device frame. Use the visual language described here, taking inspiration from a cozy miniature isometric garden reference: gentle 2D orthographic diorama, restrained painterly texture, readable large silhouettes, warm diffuse daylight from upper left, no heavy outlines. Original composition, much less micro-detail than typical AI garden illustration. Render a believable app screen, not concept art. Canvas proportions approximately 390:844. UI is clean contemporary Flutter-like cream #FBF8F0, dark green #2B3C32 text, sage action #63845B, very rounded cards, simple line icons, sans-serif. Top: title 'Potager' and compact '4 286 pas aujourd’hui'. Main central garden viewport occupies over half the vertical space and shows ONLY the potager zone of a single continuous garden, with a subtle common path/edge hinting at adjacent flower garden on left and orchard on right. Exactly eight distinct raised wooden planting beds in two loose columns of four, all visible and separated by pale stone paths; a controlled mix of tomato, carrot and zucchini in visibly different growth stages, including an empty purchased bed, a just-seeded bed, a tiny sprout, and one clearly harvestable mature tomato. Keep touch targets visually clear. A small discreet harvest-ready UI marker above only one plant. At most one small bench or watering can as decor and a few controlled grass clumps. Warm brown soil, sage grass, cream stone, earthy wood, muted red tomato accent. No water resource counter, no fantasy ornament, no glows, no plastic 3D, no pixel art, no hyperdetailed flowers, no crowding. Left and right arrow UI controls at middle sides of garden. Under garden: one clear rounded action button 'Planter'. Bottom: minimal tab navigation 'Jardin', 'Carnet', 'Défis', 'Profil'. Keep text legible and accurate. This is a production-oriented visual example of an app screen, not modular sprites.

> Edit target: the attached Growstep potager screen mockup. Preserve the portrait mobile UI layout, all French text, eight beds, vegetable types, arrow controls, and action button. Change ONLY the central garden world art so it obeys the accepted Growstep 2D isometric art bible: every planting bed has a consistent 2:1 isometric diamond top (clearly visible diagonal sides), all paths and ground tiles share that same 2:1 grid, flat orthographic camera with no photographic perspective convergence. Shorten the front-facing wooden walls of beds to low raised edges. Simplify the foliage, remove excessive background flower and bush detail, make overall world feel like readable modular sprites assembled in a tiny 2D diorama rather than a painterly 3D illustration. Keep the top-left diffuse light, warm sage palette, exactly two columns of four beds, and clean contemporary cream UI. Do not add or remove objects or text.
